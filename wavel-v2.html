GNSS-DENIED

A 100-TOPS edge inference module continuously fuses stereo VIO, tactical-grade IMU, terrain-relative DEM matching and AI target classification to maintain lock through GPS-jammed airspace — the exact condition Shahed-class engagements occur in.
